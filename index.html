Rules & controls

Each player takes five shots. Choose left, center or right, then throw a skip, lob or power shot.

Goals decide the shootout. A win earns three standings points and a tie earns one.

Campus: use WASD or arrow keys to move, Shift to sprint, and E to mount the bike.

Shooting practice

Try each shot in the 3D pool. A skip bounces off the water, a lob arcs over the keeper, and a power shot travels fast and flat.
